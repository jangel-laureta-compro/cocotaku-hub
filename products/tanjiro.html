Demon Slayer: Tanjiro Kamado Figure

This 20cm PVC scale figure captures Tanjiro Kamado in his iconic Demon Slayer uniform, complete with his signature haori pattern and Nichirin blade. Perfect for display on a shelf or desk, this piece is a must-have for any Demon Slayer collector. Official releases (Banpresto/Bandai) feature finer paint detail and sturdier joints compared to bootleg/scale versions, which is reflected in the price difference.
